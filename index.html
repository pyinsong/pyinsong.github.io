About Me

I'm a data scientist with hands-on experience building credit risk and underwriting models in consumer financial services. My work spans the full model lifecycle — from raw feature engineering on bureau trade-line data to production deployment, monitoring, and governance.

I specialize in gradient-boosted tree models (XGBoost, LightGBM) for underwriting, response, and approval decisions using alternative FCRA credit data. I care deeply about model explainability (SHAP), regulatory compliance (ECOA, FCRA, adverse action), and post-deployment drift monitoring to keep models healthy in production.

I hold an M.Eng. in Operations Research & Information Engineering from Cornell University and a B.S. in Industrial & Systems Engineering from Virginia Tech.

📁 Ballistic Trajectory Solver

Physics-based long-range ballistic calculator built from first principles. Solves the 6-DOF point-mass ODE using RK4 / scipy.integrate.solve_ivp, incorporating G7 drag models, atmospheric correction (temperature, altitude, humidity), spin drift, and Coriolis effects. Results validated against JBM Ballistics reference tables. Outputs scope adjustments in MOA and MIL via an interactive Plotly Dash web app.
